## Against the predictions and the reading rules
1. Model A (Memory_4 fit): rate within 30 percent at 0 of 4 groups at every size: MISSED. Model B (F123 sharing form): at 0 / 1 / 1 / 2 of 4 groups: MISSED. Measured c(16) / c(1) 0.17 / 0.17 / 0.18 / 0.26 against A 0.59 / 0.48 / 0.42 / 0.38 and B 0.95 / 0.77 / 0.56 / 0.47: the sharing term is not right in kind (the direction is right, the size is not: measured floors at the coherent rate, the models floor at 58 to 67, 3 to 5 times higher, and the model's drop is smooth where the measurement has a knee).
2. H (the unit does identical lanes' work once, cost linear in distinct rays per warp): within 30 percent at 3 of 4 groups at 2 of 4 sizes (1 / 3 / 4 / 2), the rule needs 3 sizes: NOT SUPPORTED by the rule as written. The measured shape is not linear: a step.
3. Rule 3 (flat from g = 1 to 16): no (down to 0.17 to 0.26). Rule 4 (c(16) within 15 percent of c(32)): HELD at all four sizes (c(16) / c(32) 0.86, 1.03, 0.87, 1.10): the cost stays at the coherent floor with 2 distinct rays per warp.
4. The knee: the cost is high with many distinct rays per warp and falls to the floor below a size dependent number: 1,000: between 32 and 16 distinct rays (63.5 to 18.6); 10,000: between 16 and 8 (76.4 to 22.9); 100,000: 8 distinct halves it (44.0), 4 reaches 21.1; 1,000,000: 8 distinct 64.7, 4 distinct 28.3. Distinct nodes fetched per warp at the last high point: 1,000: 32 x 6 = 192; 10,000: 16 x 9 = 144; 100,000: 16 x 12 = 192 (76.3), 8 x 12 = 96 (44.0); 1,000,000: 8 x 15 = 120 (64.7), 4 x 15 = 60 (28.3). A limit near 100 to 190 distinct node requests per warp fits, but it was not tested (the numbers move with counts and size; the fit is by eye over 4 scenes): a hypothesis only.
5. One warp latency does not depend on g (1.0 to 1.07): lanes of a warp are served in parallel by the unit, and the warp finishes when its slowest lane does.

## Reading
- The 4.5 to 5.9 times of F197 (rate, distinct over identical lanes) reproduces with equal counts (design B g = 1 over g = 32: 5.0 / 5.9 / 4.8 / 4.3 times; F197 4.9 / 4.5 / 5.9 / 4.5), so it is not the slowest lane effect. It is a property of how many distinct rays a warp holds, with a knee: below it the unit runs at the identical lane rate, above it about 4 to 5 times slower per ray. Identical lane work is NOT simply done once (H fails its rule), but sharing helps a lot once the distinct work per warp falls under a limit.
- The 2.7 to 3.3 times at one warp of F197 is withdrawn as a lane parallelism result: it is the warp's slowest lane (F67 of the slowest ray 0.76 to 1.03 of measured; same counts: 0.97 to 1.07 of the F67 line).
- Not determined: what the knee is a limit of (distinct node requests, distinct lines, distinct rays; cache or unit state), its dependence on warps (all runs 32 warps with the same 32 rays in every warp), and whether distinct rays on one shared path (F28) are free.
