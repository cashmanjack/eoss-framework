# The current performance model on every validation so far (2026-11-17, no new run, no refit)

Generated by model/model_table.py from the numbers in the cited results files. Current model = F123 form for ray order speedups; one SM fit plus sharing for absolute traversal time; for custom primitive programs the max of the throughput form and the slowest warp chain (F219) and, as a second column, the measured callback cost per program and input (F216 (b)). The two prior works forms were NOT combined into one new form for this table (that would need its own prediction committed first).
Metrics: speedup rows = rms log error of the predicted speedup of a ray order over the natural order, and the no change baseline (predict 1.0; for the F123 and F205 rows the baseline is the spread, the rms log of the measured speedups, which is the same thing: the score of predicting 1.0 everywhere). Absolute time rows = ratio measured / predicted and median absolute natural log error (0.69 = a factor 2; there is no no change baseline for an absolute time). "blind" = the prediction was committed before the measurement and nothing was refitted afterwards.

## C. What the table says

- Speedups of ray orders: the model is useful where the order matters (gain over 10 percent): errors 0.03 to 0.13 log against 0.15 to 0.70 for no change. It is not better than no change where the gain is small (Sponza), and it has a known miss on shuffled camera order and on Rungholt oracle orders. 10 of 12 sets better; 7 of 9 blind sets.
- Absolute time is the weak part. One SM, same kind of scene: 11 to 19 percent. Whole GPU: 16 to 89 percent. Custom primitive programs on real data: median factor 2 (RTNN, TrueKNN with the chain) to 3.5 (Arkade), with single rows 100 to 160 times off (TrueKNN k 659 and Porto with measured callbacks).
- Max of throughput and slowest warp helps only TrueKNN (2.82 to 0.69), by construction not RTNN or Arkade. Measured callback cost helps TrueKNN (2.82 to 1.05) and RTNN a little (0.86 to 0.69), Arkade fixed (1.53 to 1.03) but it makes single rows worse (TrueKNN road 3.1 to 4.8, Porto 91 to 128). Neither form is a validated absolute predictor; the right claim is a speedup predictor plus an absolute estimate within about a factor 2 to 4 for custom primitive launches when its program is calibrated.
- Points never predicted blind in the table (not scored as blind): F123 sets, Memory_4 held out groups, Throughput_6, the (b) and F219 forms, F220.
- Not in the table (no time prediction by the model, mechanism or structure measurements): cache and scheduling tests, flags and masks, build flags and quantization, spheres (F204 saturation slopes predicted 2 to 5 times too high, a different form), HG_1 per call overhead, unit and knee thread (F194 to F201 are fits and locators), bandwidth bound (F217: not a bound).
