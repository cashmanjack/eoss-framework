Stage 0 (largest scene, shifted start file, 32 warps, no TTU) clean, smallest TTU launch (soup:1,000 shifted, 1 warp) clean, 120 runs, all exit 0, one block each, no fault, GPU idle after. One run attempt stopped at its first launch (W = 12 with a 1,024 ray file: the harness needs a multiple of 32 x W rays; no TTU code ran, files for W = 12 made with 384 rays, nothing else changed). Analysis model/analyze_knee2.py, notes/unit3g/analysisK2.txt (private).
Hypothesis K: knee where the MEAN distinct node lines per step per warp = 10.2, unit bound regime only (16 or more warps).

## 1. New sizes (soup:30,000 and 300,000, W = 16 and 32): HELD, 4 of 4 within a factor 2
Knee g measured / predicted: 30,000 W16 2.41 / 2.69, W32 3.08 / 2.69; 300,000 W16 3.10 / 2.81, W32 4.13 / 2.81. The knee keeps rising with size (W = 32: 1.5, 2.9, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1, 4.3 for 1,000 to 1,000,000), the prediction only rises 2.1 to 2.9 (it compresses the trend, as stated).

## 2. W = 12: MISSED (predicted no knee at 4 of 6 sizes; measured a knee at 5 of 6)
top / floor at W = 12: 1.07 (1,000), 2.01, 2.21, 2.72, 3.03, 2.70 (10,000 to 1,000,000), knees 1.4 to 3.0. The switch to the unit bound regime lies between 8 warps (flat, F200) and 12, not at 16; at soup:1,000 it is still flat at W = 12 (1.07).

## 3. Shifted variant (same distinct rays, half the lanes of each group one step behind): NOT HELD by the rule
Scored 12 configurations (100,000 / 300,000 / 1,000,000, g = 4, 8, 16, W = 16, 32, where K and the distinct ray alternative differ by more than 25 percent): within 25 percent of K in 6, closer to K than to the alternative in 5 (rule: 9 of 12 and a majority). The pattern: at g = 4 (8 distinct rays, shifted D_mean 13.7 to 14.4) the shifted cost is HIGH (W = 32: 75.5, 76.6, 78.3, the same as plain g = 2 and above plain g = 4: 37.5, 52.6, 54.6): K's side; at g = 8 (4 distinct rays, shifted D_mean about 7.1) it is LOW (19.7, 23.8, 26.9, equal to plain g = 8: 20.9, 21.6, 25.7): the distinct ray side. At the same D_mean 7.1 the plain g = 4 costs 37 to 55 and the shifted g = 8 costs 20 to 27. So the mean distinct lines per step does not determine the cost by itself (nor do distinct rays): a threshold near 10 to 14 lines per step fits the shifted points (high above, low below) but the plain g = 4 points (D_mean 7.1) are in between. Small sizes (not scored): the child start removes a third to a half of the work there and the results are mostly flat.

## Reading
