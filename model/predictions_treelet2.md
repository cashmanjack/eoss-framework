# Treelet_2 predictions, tethys (RTX 4090 GPU 1), 2026-11-24. Written BEFORE any arm was timed at scale. OptiX only (no TTU code of ours is run), no process matching by name.

Follows Treelet_1 (F437, model/predictions_treelet1.md). Program ttu/bench/Treelet_2 (treelet2.cu, treelet2_dev.cu, run2.py). Same persistent kernel (v5, exact in Treelet_1), same arms for the presorted comparison; new: end to end arms with the grouping cost inside the timed span, Morton baselines, a box inspector, a real scene.

What was run before this file (setup, no arm timing at scale): a 200,000 triangle smoke of every arm (all exact, 0 wrong; its inspector numbers were: key accuracy 0.51, 65 percent of rays failed verification; timings of a 65,536 ray set seen but not used); treelet size queries; ray generation. Fresh soup: 8,000,000 triangles, scene seed 99 (Treelet_1 used 1234), ray seed 99 + 777 / + 4242, grid 14 (2,744 treelets, GAS median 120,704 B, full GAS 382 MB = 5.3 times the 72 MB L2): set f_rand 7,942,464 hit rays, f_b3 (depth 3 bounce, from random primaries) 7,278,848. Real scene: Rungholt (casual-effects.com archive, 6,704,264 triangles, kept in /dev/shm, never in git; full GAS 139 MB = 1.9 times L2, NOT well past L2: the largest real scene I have is this one), treelets = 6,000 consecutive triangles in Morton order of the centroid (1,118 treelets, GAS median 118,656 B, tight boxes), camera at (-260, 60, -240) looking at (0, 8, 20), 65 degrees, 8192 x 4096 primaries, depth 3 cosine bounce rays, natural order = pixel order with dead rays dropped: set rg_cam3x 5,730,048 hit rays (open scene: only 23 percent of the primaries survive three bounces; a 4096 x 2048 version gave only 1.4 M rays and is not used).

Definitions. Presorted arms (as Treelet_1, rays already in the arm's order, timing = the launch): nat_R, grp_R, grp_T, pg_T, pp_T. End to end arms (timing = everything on the GPU from the natural order rays to the results back in the natural order: keys, CUB radix sort, gather, launches, scatter, treelet to triangle id mapping): e_mo4, e_mo5 (Morton of the ray origin, 4 or 5 bits per axis, root start, normal launch); e_ogR (oracle treelet key, free, sort, root start); e_ogT, e_opT (oracle key, sort, treelet start grouped normal launch / pinned persistent queue per TPC); e_igT, e_ipT (inspector, see below). WPS = persistent warps per SM; the blind setting is 16 (every SM full), sensitivity 8. 10 rounds, rotated arm order, median of 3 processes per cell (range reported). Every result of every arm is checked against the full GAS closest hit (reference from the prep process).
Inspector (Liu, Hegde, Kulkarni ICS 2016 style, top level only): one custom primitive per treelet (its tight bounding box plus 1e-4 of the scene diagonal), a closest hit trace of every ray against the boxes gives the treelet to queue it in (nearest box entry; ties at equal entry distance broken toward the box whose centre is nearest to the entry point). The ray is then traced in that treelet's GAS only. Exactness: a second box trace (same boxes, the assigned box skipped, tmax = the hit distance in the treelet, or the whole ray if it missed) finds any other box entered before the hit; rays that fail it are traced again from the root of the full GAS (grouped by the inspector key, fallback launch over the compacted list). Inspector cost = the first box trace plus the verification trace plus the fallback, all inside the timed span, reported per stage. Key accuracy = share of rays whose inspector key equals the oracle treelet of the closest hit.

## Item 1, blind confirmation (fresh scene and rays: f_rand, f_b3, WPS 16, presorted arms)
1a. pp_T / grp_T (speed ratio, median of 3 processes): between 1.15 and 1.45 on both sets: 75 percent; central 1.30; below 1.10 on either set: 10 percent. The two sets within 0.10 of each other: 70 percent.
1b. pp_T / pg_T between 1.6 and 2.2: 70 percent. grp_R / nat_R between 3.6 and 4.6: 70 percent (Treelet_1: 3.96 to 4.10). grp_T / grp_R between 1.8 and 2.6: 70 percent (F437: 2.2).
1c. pp_T absolute time f_rand between 0.95 and 1.25 ms: 65 percent (Treelet_1: 1.05 on 8.03 M rays).
1d. At WPS 8 (sensitivity, same runs repeated): pp_T / grp_T below 1.10 on both sets: 70 percent.
Decision rule: Treelet_1's WPS 16 result is "confirmed blind" if pp_T / grp_T is at least 1.15 on BOTH f_rand and f_b3 at WPS 16; "not confirmed" if below 1.10 on either; between: "partial". Wrong rays: 0 in every arm: 95 percent.

## Item 2, fair baselines with the grouping cost (f_rand, f_b3, WPS 16)
2a. Overhead of a sort based scheme (key, sort of 8 M pairs, gather, scatter back): 1.8 to 3.2 ms: 65 percent (central 2.4). Sorting 15 bit keys costs no more than 12 bit keys by more than 0.4 ms: 70 percent.
2b. e_mo5 end to end between 5.0 and 7.5 ms (central 6.0): 60 percent; its speedup over nat_R (about 11.9 ms) between 1.6 and 2.4: 60 percent. e_mo4 within 10 percent of e_mo5: 75 percent.
2c. e_opT (oracle key free, sort and everything else paid) between 3.2 and 4.8 ms: 60 percent; speedup over nat_R between 2.5 and 3.8: 65 percent; over e_mo5 between 1.3 and 2.0: 60 percent; over e_ogT (same, not pinned) between 1.00 and 1.15: 60 percent (the sort cost swamps the 0.3 ms pinning saves).
2d. The sort and key cost is larger than the whole pinned trace: 90 percent.
Decision rule: "pinned treelets pay end to end against natural order and against a Morton sort" only if e_opT is at least 1.2 times faster than both nat_R and e_mo5 on both f_rand and f_b3, and I say explicitly that the oracle key is free there (a lower bound on the cost); the honest, non oracle comparison is item 3.

Decision rule: "the inspector scheme pays" only if e_ipT is at least 1.2 times faster than nat_R end to end on both soup sets; "competitive" only if also within 1.2 times of e_mo5. Anything else: reported as measured.
