# Cache_1 / Cache_2: results (2026-11-17, tethys GPU 1, RTX 4090, plain CUDA, no TTU; predictions model/predictions_cache.md committed first, 3ba55c0)

Program ttu/bench/Cache_1/cache1.cu, runner run.py. GPU 1 idle (0 percent, no process) before each mode, all exit 0. Raw notes/cache1 (private). L1 hit 40 cycles, L2 285 (F176 had 41 for L1: this chase measures 40), DRAM not used.

## L1 (loads through L1, no shared memory in use)
- Sector 32 bytes: sequential cyclic over 1 MB, stride 4 / 8 / 16 / 32: 70 / 101 / 163 / 285 cycles (miss fraction stride / 32), random order 279 to 285 at every stride.
- Capacity (stride 128, sequential cyclic): latency 40 up to 88 KB, 89 at 92 KB, 122 at 96, 172 at 104, 250 at 112, 285 from 128 KB. The midpoint (163) is crossed at 100 KB (strides 32 and 64) and 104 KB (stride 128); no miss up to 88 KB, full miss from 124 to 128 KB. Between 104 and 120 KB the scan misses 85 to 89 percent (it is not the 100 percent of a plain LRU over capacity), so the effective capacity is about 96 KB with a soft edge of about 36 KB (88 to 124 KB), not a sharp 128 KB. Random cyclic order: the same onset (92 KB) and a slower rise (254 to 264 at 128 to 140 KB, still not 285).
- Tag line 128 bytes: the knee at stride 32, 64 and 128 is at the same footprint (100 / 100 / 104 KB). At stride 256 the knee is at 204 KB (2.0 times the footprint, the same number of lines: 800 lines at every stride): a power of two stride does not lose capacity to set conflicts.
- Associativity: NOT found. Up to 128 addresses at strides from 128 bytes to 1 MB the latency stays at 40 (no conflict knee, so more than 128 ways or a hashed set index); with 600 to 1,100 lines at strides 128 B to 128 KB the knee is at the same 700 to 1,000 lines for every stride, i.e. the capacity, not a way count. A way count is therefore not measurable with this method on this L1. Replacement: not measurable the same way (no conflict set); the capacity edge above (partial miss over capacity, soft edge) is not LRU like but it cannot name the policy.
## L2 (.cg loads)
- Associativity: not found: m up to 160 at strides 4 KB to 2 MB shows no knee (latency 276 to 298 everywhere); no set conflicts below the cache size (hashed set and slice index or more than 160 ways).
- Per line latency over 16 MB (one SM, warm, one dependent load timed per line, random and ascending order): 291 to 352 cycles, median 319, 100 percent of lines within 15 percent of the median, per line values reproduce between two different orders (r = 0.95, 100,362 lines), and the ascending order shows no dependence on address (318 mean in every 4,096 line block). The distribution has several levels (peaks near 297, 305, 310, 319, 325, 331, 341, 6 to 11 cycles apart), not two modes: no near and far partition, but a graded per line spread of about 50 cycles (17 percent) that is a property of the line (slice distance), not of the address range.
- Artifact found and excluded: the first two l2lat runs showed exactly 1 in 8 accesses at 575 to 620 cycles. It depends on the position in the access order (every 8th timed access, whichever line, overlap of the slow sets of two seeds at chance), it is the access order array's sector fetch inside the timed region (fixed by waiting for it before the clock read, which left the artifact at the same positions, so the cause is not fully removed); the statistics above use the other 7 in 8 positions. Raw files of both runs kept.
