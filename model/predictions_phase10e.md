## 2. F243 close out: L2 hit rate dose response and one curve test
Own TTU kernel beside a stream whose lines are uniformly random over a footprint of F MB (new stream type l2fp<F>, 32 lines per warp load, every lane its own 128 B line, ld.global.cg), random rays (soup 100000), 4090 (72 MB L2). Footprints 40, 56, 72, 96, 120 MB (the hit rate falls from about 1 to about 0.6 as F passes 72 MB; not measured, footprint is the proxy), each unthrottled and with a 150 us sleep per iteration; reference arms dramfew (256 MB) unthrottled and with the sleep. Placement sib (4 blocks per SM on the TPC siblings of the RT SMs) and high (2 per SM, other TPCs). Three repetitions, alone run before every arm, clock sampled in every arm (clksamp). Only random rays; coherent rays not run.
Measured per arm: RT ratio r = rays/us with the stream over alone (same repetition); stream line rate lambda = counted iterations x 12,288 lines per iteration / window; stream load latency l = mean over timed loads (lane 0 of every warp, every 16th iteration, first load of the iteration, cycles from issue to consumption, SM cycles over the sampled clock); outstanding occupancy O = lambda x l (lines in flight), also per streaming SM.
Hypothesis (Jack): one curve r(O) for L2 and DRAM streams alike, i.e. a shared per TPC pool of outstanding misses.
Rule, fixed now: HOLDS if on sib (a) r falls monotonically with O over all arms, pooled Spearman at most -0.9, and (b) every pair of an L2 arm (F <= 72) and a DRAM arm (F >= 96, dramfew) whose O differs by less than 1.5x have r within 0.10 absolute. Otherwise it does not hold; then I say which part failed.
My prediction (skeptical): latency rises with footprint (about 2 to 3 times from F 40 to dramfew); r on sib falls with footprint unthrottled (about 0.08 at 40, 0.05 at 96, 0.03 at 120); rank correlation of r with O at most -0.8 (a monotone trend exists), but the single curve rule FAILS at (b): at equal O the DRAM arms cost more than the L2 arms by more than 0.10 (page spread, F243's 2.5x, is a factor independent of O, and the L2 arms here have 20 to 60 distinct pages per window). Probability the rule holds: 30 percent. On high (other TPCs) r is higher than on sib at equal O (the pool would be per TPC, so other TPCs should not share it): 85 percent.
Known confound, disclosed before running: footprint also changes the pages per warp load (about 14 at 40 MB, about 23 at 120 MB with 2 MB pages), and the 150 us sleep arms run at lower lambda with the same latency.
