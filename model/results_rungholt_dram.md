Verdict: the pass rule (leave one out rms at most 0.06, Spearman at least 0.8, beats both T3 regressors, Morton within 0.04) FAILS on all four counts. So "the missing DRAM term explains the Rungholt under prediction" is NOT supported by the replay's DRAM feature, and the feature itself is not usable as a DRAM traffic estimate here.
