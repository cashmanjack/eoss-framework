Facts read from it: (1) the writes (268 to 270 MB) are the ray results (32 B per ray) and the ray input reads another 268 MB: 538 MB of ray traffic that every order pays and that neither the replay feature nor the model has (0.53 ms at the full 1,008 GB/s). (2) The natural order is DRAM bandwidth bound: 85.8 percent of peak, DRAM bytes (read + write) / (bandwidth x time) = 0.84. (3) Sorting cuts the DRAM reads 2.45 times at once (985 to 401 MB at oracle depth 1) and then not at all: depth 1 to full read 401, 401, 402, 402, 402 MB (about 133 MB beyond the ray input) while the time keeps falling 1.147 to 0.884 ms and the DRAM share of peak rises from 59 to 77 percent. (4) The F209 feature over estimates the bytes (measured read over feature 0.27 to 0.70), as F209 inferred from the bandwidth bound: its D per order fell to 576 MB at full order where the measured read stayed at 402.
Scoring of my written expectations: E1 (read below the feature for natural, d1, d2, d3; natural ratio 0.3 to 0.8) HELD (0.46, 0.27, 0.31, 0.33). E2 (natural read / (bandwidth x time) 0.25 to 0.85, at most 1.0 everywhere) HELD (0.66; all at most 0.66 for reads); my 35 percent case "at least 60 percent of peak = bandwidth bound" HAPPENED (85.8 percent). E3 (natural over full between 1.5 and 4) HELD (2.45); monotone fall with depth MISSED (flat from depth 1: 401 to 402 MB). E4 (the F209 rule with measured bytes) FAILED: Spearman 0.10 (needed 0.8), Morton residual after the fit -0.075 (needed within 0.04); the leave one out rms of 0.028 and the win over the order index and miss regressors are an artifact: x = log(natural / order) is the same 0.897 to 0.898 for all five oracle orders, so the fit is a constant offset (slope 0.159 x 0.897 = 0.14, the mean residual) and says nothing about the shape across depths. Not counted as a pass.
