# RTX 5090 (pc14), session 8 predictions, 2026-11-19. Written BEFORE any run of tethys's ttu/bench/L2rw on this GPU (plain CUDA, no TTU, no OptiX).

Disclosure: I have read the 4090 results (F423 to F425, model/results_4090_batch7.md) and my own 5090 stream benchmark (F421: write only half the read rate, 512 byte coalesced 16 B accesses, mixes above read only). The 4090 values I compare with (F423): read only 5.05 TB/s and write only 2.0 TB/s for the one sector per lane pattern (cost ratio 2.5), 3.9 and 2.6 TB/s coalesced 128 B (ratio 1.5), partial 4 B writes the same per sector as full sector writes (2.51 against 2.57), footprints agree within 8 percent, hit rate 99 to 100 percent, mixes cheaper than linear (50 percent writes 13 to 22 percent below the mean of the two ends).
Run: `run_l2rw.py BIN OUTDIR 8,32,80` (patterns 0 coalesced 128 B, 1 lane4 = one sector per lane with 4 B accesses, 2 lane32 = one sector per lane with 32 B accesses; write quarters 0 to 4; 3 repetitions, ncu on repetition 0), built with nvcc -O3 -arch=sm_120. The script's idle test (any compute process, which includes the Steam helper on this machine) is replaced by notes/pc14_gpucheck.sh in a pc14 copy (ttu/bench/pc14/run_l2rw_pc14.py), nothing else changed; every process under timeout (the script's own 60 s and 120 s), GPU 0, TMPDIR in the workspace.
Cost ratio c_w / c_r = time per sector of write only over read only, same pattern and footprint. Rates are TB/s of requested bytes as the program prints them.
Predictions:
1. L2 hit rate (ncu) at least 98 percent at 8 and 32 MB in every config: 85 percent; at 80 MB at least 90 percent: 55 percent (the 5090's ramp started at 0.94 of 96 MB in F403, so 80 MB should still hit).
2. Read only rate, coalesced pattern (0): between 4.5 and 6.8 TB/s: 55 percent. Read only, lane32 (2): between 4.5 and 7.5 TB/s: 50 percent. Both above the 4090's (3.9 and 5.05): 80 percent.
3. Write cost ratio, coalesced (0): between 1.5 and 2.5 (my stream benchmark: 2.0; the 4090: 1.5): 65 percent. Lane32 (2): between 1.5 and 3.0: 60 percent; at least 2.5 (the 4090's): 35 percent; below 1.5: 15 percent.
4. Partial sector writes (1) cost per sector within 15 percent of the full sector writes (2): 55 percent.
5. Footprints 8, 32 and 80 MB: rates within 10 percent of each other (same pattern and write share) at 8 and 32 MB: 70 percent; at 80 MB within 15 percent of the 32 MB value: 60 percent.
6. Mixes: the 50 percent write mix (wq 2) is faster per sector than the linear (time) mean of the two ends by 10 to 30 percent in the coalesced and lane32 patterns: 55 percent.
7. ncu L2 throughput percent of peak is 90 to 100 for read only and for write only alike (a bottleneck reading, as on the 4090): 65 percent.
8. Whether the kNN speedup method of F425 (byte mix times per sector costs) predicts the 5090 better with these lane costs than with the coalesced ones: not predicted (it is a scoring step on measured numbers; the speedups are known).
