# Knee map (F199 follow up): predictions, written 2026-11-17 BEFORE any run (tethys GPU 1, RTX 4090)

Hypothesis H (Jack): per ray cost depends on the distinct node lines a warp requests per step against a capacity C; above C the cost is the distinct ray rate, below it the identical lane floor.
Design: design B of F199 (32 rays of one (nodes, slots, triangles) class per size: 6/3/5, 9/3/7, 12/4/9, 15/5/10; groups of g = 1 2 4 8 16 32 consecutive lanes share a ray; the same rays, they are regenerated from the same seeds and class rule), the checked Unit_1 build (no new TTU code), 32 warps replaced by W = 4, 8, 16, 32 warps (every warp runs the same 32 rays, as in F199), 30 launches, c = span / (32 W) cycles per ray per SM. 4 sizes x 6 g x 4 W = 96 runs.
Inputs (CPU replay, model/replay NODESEQ, model/knee_inputs.py): the node fetch sequence of every ray; for a warp, D(g) by statistic: step_max (the maximum over steps k of the distinct node ids among the lanes' k-th fetches, the primary form of H), step_mean, total (distinct node ids over the whole traversal). Result table notes/unit3g/knee_inputs.json (also in model/predictions_knee_table.txt). The step based D is almost exactly 32 / g at every size (28 to 32 at g = 1), so H as stated predicts a size independent knee.
Knee: for each (size, W): top = c(1), floor = c(32), a knee exists if top / floor >= 1.5, g_knee = where c(g) crosses (top + floor) / 2, linear in c on a log2 g axis.
Calibration from ONE configuration: soup:100,000 at 32 warps, using the F199 design B numbers (85.9 76.3 44.0 21.1 15.6 18.0): g_knee 3.37, C = D(g_knee): H step_max 9.5, step_mean 8.3, total 96.5; xW variants (demand = W x D, capacity from W = 32): 303.6 and 3,087.5.

DISCLOSURE: the knees at W = 32 for the other three sizes were already visible in F199 (design B c(g): about 1.5, 3.0, 5.0 for 1,000, 10,000, 1,000,000), so the W = 32 comparison is not blind; W = 4, 8, 16 are blind. C is calibrated only on soup:100,000, W = 32.
Reading rules (set before the run): (1) a statistic PREDICTS THE KNEE if over the 15 scored configurations (all except the calibration one) its g_knee is within a factor 2 of the measured one (or both are 'none') in at least 12, AND (2) its size trend is right: if the measured g_knee varies by more than a factor 2 across the four sizes at W = 32 while the statistic's spread is under 1.5, it fails the trend (so H step_max cannot pass if the measured knee moves with size as in F199). (3) If no statistic passes, what the knee tracks: for each statistic the spread (standard deviation of log2 D at the measured knee) over all configurations that have a knee; the smallest spread is what the knee tracks, reported as post hoc; a spread under 0.5 in log2 counts as tracking.
Probabilities (mine): H step_max passes (1) and (2): 0.05 (it cannot follow the size trend); step_mean 0.05; total 0.25; step_max x W 0.1; total x W 0.1; some statistic of the five passes 0.4. Probability that the knee depends on W at all (measured g_knee at W = 4 differs from W = 32 by more than a factor 2 or disappears): 0.5. Probability that a floor c(32) is similar at all W (within 30 percent per ray): 0.3.
