# Treelet_1 predictions, tethys (RTX 4090 GPU 1), 2026-11-24. Written BEFORE any arm was timed. OptiX only (no TTU code of ours is run).

Test (kill test): do treelet queues pinned to one TPC (persistent warps placed by %smid) beat the same rays grouped by treelet and launched normally, on a large incoherent workload? Program ttu/bench/Treelet_1 (treelet1.cu, treelet1_dev.cu). Proxy for "start at the treelet root": one GAS per treelet (oracle key: the treelet holding the ray's closest hit, as Memory_4; every result is checked against the full GAS closest hit).

What was run before this file (setup, no arm timing): a 200,000 triangle smoke of prep and tlsize; prep of the 8,000,000 triangle soup (same generator as Morton, seed 1234; full GAS 387.4 MB compacted = 5.4 times the 72 MB L2 of the 4090) and the treelet size query. Treelets K = 14 (14^3 = 2,744 cells by triangle centroid, treelet id = Morton rank of the cell): 2,915 triangles, GAS 110,976 / 120,704 / 140,160 bytes (min / median / max), 332 MB in all (4.6 times L2). K = 15 gives median 97,792 bytes. K = 14 (median 121 KB, about the 130 KB target) is the run size. Rays (8,388,608 generated each): rand = random origin in the cube, uniform direction, 8,028,960 hit rays kept (2,926 per treelet); b3 = depth 3 bounce rays of the same random primaries (cosine weighted, origin at the hit point), 7,401,216 hit rays kept (2,697 per treelet). Natural order = generation order with misses removed (random, no pixel order). Only hit rays are used in every arm.

Arms (LAYOUT_START). Layout nat = natural order, one launch index per ray; grp = rays sorted by treelet (Morton rank), same launch; pn = persistent warps over one global queue in natural order (control for the persistent machinery); pg = persistent, one global queue, grouped order; pp = persistent, grouped order, 64 queues (one per TPC, treelets split into 64 contiguous runs of equal ray count), each warp reads %smid and takes the queue of TPC smid / 2, steals from the next non empty queue only when its own is empty. START R = full GAS from the root; T = the ray's own treelet GAS. 10 arms per ray set. Persistent launch = 128 SMs x WPS warps; WPS = argmin time of pg_R on rand over {8, 16, 24, 32} (3 rounds, ties to the smaller); pg and pp use the same WPS. Every launch is preceded by the same 268 MB memset of the output buffer (equal L2 state for all arms). Timing: CUDA events around optixLaunch, 10 rounds, arm order rotated every round, median; SM clock read after every launch (NVML). ncu: TMPDIR on a directory we own, --cache-control none --clock-control none, two single pass metric groups (as Memory_4b), one warm launch then the profiled launch per arm. RT L1 hit rate = (l1tex__t_sectors_lookup_hit - lsu hit) / (l1tex__t_sectors - lsu).

Predictions (speed ratio = rays per second ratio = time ratio inverted):
1. Correctness: every arm, both sets: 0 wrong hit or primitive (95 percent); t bit identical to the full GAS reference for every ray (85 percent).
2. Grouping, root start (F96: oracle grouping 2.8 times over a shuffled launch at 8M triangles on the 6000 Ada): grp_R / nat_R between 2.0 and 4.0 on both sets: 65 percent; central 2.8. Below 1.5: 10 percent.
3. Treelet start against root start, same grouped order: grp_T / grp_R between 1.4 and 3.5: 65 percent, central 2.0 (the separate GAS skips the upper 3 to 4 levels and the overlapping sibling subtrees; Memory_4 ray starts gave 2 to 5 times). nat_T / nat_R between 1.3 and 3.0: 60 percent.
4. Persistent machinery cost: pn_R / nat_R and pg_R / grp_R each between 0.85 and 1.10: 65 percent (central 0.97); persistent beating normal launch by more than 10 percent: 15 percent.
5. THE QUESTION. pp_T / grp_T on rand between 1.00 and 1.30: 60 percent, central 1.10; at or above 1.15: 35 percent; below 1.00: 20 percent. Same ranges for pp_T / pg_T. b3 equal to rand within 0.07 in the ratio: 60 percent. Root start: pp_R / grp_R between 0.97 and 1.15: 65 percent, central 1.04.
6. RT L1 hit rate (ncu): nat_R 15 to 45 percent; grp_R 40 to 70; grp_T 40 to 70; pp_T 65 to 93; pp_T minus grp_T at least 10 points: 70 percent; pp_T minus pg_T at least 10 points: 70 percent. L1 fills from L2 per ray: pp_T lower than grp_T by 25 to 60 percent (central 40): 60 percent. DRAM bytes per ray: pp_T within 15 percent of grp_T (both read each treelet once): 65 percent; grp_R below nat_R by a factor of 2 to 5: 65 percent. Time gain smaller than the L1 fill reduction (as Memory_4b: fills halved, 6 percent in time): 70 percent.
7. Pinning mechanics: stolen chunks below 5 percent of all chunks in pp: 70 percent (below 15 percent: 90 percent). Warps per SM at launch (read from %smid): min and max within WPS plus or minus 2: 65 percent. Lane mismatches (launch index and %laneid): 0.
8. WPS chosen by the sweep: 16 or 24: 60 percent.

Decision rule, fixed now. "TPC pinning is what was missing" only if on BOTH ray sets, treelet start, K = 14: pp_T is at least 1.15 times faster than grp_T AND than pg_T, AND the RT L1 hit rate of pp_T is at least 10 points above both. "Not what was missing" if pp_T is under 1.05 times the faster of grp_T and pg_T on either set (with or without a hit rate gain). Anything between is "partial, not decisive". A difference under the round to round spread of the two arms (max minus min of the 10 medians' rounds, as a ratio) counts as no difference. Root start arms are reported but do not decide.
What the earlier experiment (Memory_4, Memory_4b) can and cannot tell: see notes/reports/tethys_20261124_treelet1.md (audit). Treelets there were depths 1 to 4 of a 7 level tree, never sized to a cache, and not pinned to a TPC.
Reading rules: one dataset per set (seed fixed), one scene size; "not what was missing" would be about this scene and size only. If the proxy cannot place warps (warps per SM far from even), the pinned result is void and is reported as such.

## Addendum 1, 2026-11-24, written after the first valid main runs (timing medians seen), before the repeats and before any ncu
What happened between the first commit (5cbc0e7) and now, all disclosed: (a) the first persistent kernel (lane 0 claims 32 rays, shuffle broadcast) lost lanes in stolen chunks (pp arms: 14,083 and 2,604 wrong rays, pn and pg exact); a __syncwarp fix did not help, a global slot broadcast hung (killed by me after 120 s, OptiX only, no fault), a per lane claim (K rays per atomic) worked but cost 55 percent (pg_R 4.57 ms against 3.65), the final kernel (v5) uses fully warp uniform control flow and is exact in every arm (0 wrong, 0 t mismatches, both sets). Raw files of the invalid runs stay in /dev/shm/treelet1/runs (main_*_20261124T10053*/10064*/10065*, wpssweep before 101424 and the chunksweep_*); only files from 101521 on are valid. (b) The WPS sweep was redone with v5: WPS 8 (3.649 ms) beats 16 (3.721), 24 (3.703), 32 (3.744); my prediction 8 (16 or 24) missed. WPS above 16 puts 16 to 120 warps on some SMs (residency is 16 warps per SM), so 16 is the largest even placement.
Seen once (WPS 8, rule, v5; medians in ms): rand grp_T 1.343, pg_T 2.063, pp_T 1.328; b3 grp_T 1.293, pg_T 1.974, pp_T 1.252. So by the rule fixed above pinning is NOT what was missing (pp_T / grp_T = 1.01 and 1.03, under 1.05), although pp_T / pg_T = 1.55 and 1.58. At WPS 16 (post hoc, seen once, NOT the rule's WPS): pp_T 1.073 and 1.002, grp_T 1.411 and 1.288 (ratio 1.31 and 1.29), pg_T 2.087 and 1.877.
Why a pg arm is the wrong control for "launched normally" (reading, not measured): a normal launch hands consecutive rays to consecutive thread blocks, so a treelet's rays run on one SM at a time; my global queue hands consecutive 32 ray chunks to whichever warp asks, so a treelet's rays are spread over all 128 SMs. pg is therefore a control for the queue machinery, grp is the normal launch baseline.
Pre declared follow up (this addendum is the commit before it): (1) repeat main at WPS 8 and WPS 16, 3 processes per set and WPS, report median of the process medians and the range; WPS 16 is labeled post hoc whatever comes out. (2) ncu on rand at WPS 8 and 16 and on b3 at WPS 16, same arms, same metrics as above. (3) one treelet size sensitivity, K = 20 (8,000 treelets, about 1,000 triangles, expected 40 to 45 KB GAS), rand, WPS 8 and 16, main only. Predictions: pp_T / grp_T at WPS 16 between 1.15 and 1.45 on both sets, again: 60 percent (it was seen once); at WPS 8 below 1.08: 70 percent. The RT L1 hit rate of pp_T is at least 10 points above grp_T at WPS 16: 65 percent; at WPS 8: 40 percent. K = 20: pp_T / grp_T at WPS 16 between 1.0 and 1.4: 60 percent, and not larger than at K = 14: 55 percent (smaller treelets fit better but each gets about 1,000 rays and the root cost of each start grows relative to the work). The decision label stays the one fixed above (WPS 8, K = 14: not what was missing); the WPS 16 result is reported next to it as sensitivity, not as a replacement.
